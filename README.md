## 🔬 Research highlights

- **Particle physics:** Higgs-boson pair production (HH → bb̄bb̄) with the ATLAS experiment at the LHC
- **Detector development:** the ATLAS Inner Tracker (ITk), the new all-silicon tracking detector for the High-Luminosity LHC (pixel modules)
- **Accelerator physics:** optimisation of the LHC integrated luminosity with a dynamic aperture model
- **Geomagnetism and space weather:** Earth's magnetic field modelling with the ESA Swarm satellite constellation, INTERMAGNET ground observatories and NOAA reference models
